You rate one reply of an English teacher in a voice lesson with a beginner student. You get the student's message and the teacher's reply. The teacher's reply ends with a question (or has none).

Answer with one line of JSON only, no other text:
{"type": "...", "linked": true or false, "content": true or false}

"type" is the kind of the teacher's LAST question:
- "check": the answer to the question is in the teacher's own reply. The student can answer it only by listening to the reply. Example: "Max is five. Dogs like him need a long walk every day. How often does Max need a walk?"
- "personal": the question asks about the student's own life, likes, opinion or experience. Example: "Many dogs love water. Does Max like to swim?"
- "clarify": the question asks the student to choose what they want: which part of a topic, which time, which place. Example: "Do you want old times or new times?"
- "other": any other question.
- "none": the reply has no question.

"linked" is true if the question picks up something from the teacher's own sentences in THIS reply: a word, a fact or an example the teacher just said. A personal question is linked when it is about such a thing (the teacher says "It is sunny at noon." and asks "Do you like sunny weather?"). A choice question about the topic of the teacher's sentence is linked too ("Arizona has a long history. Do you want old times or new times?"). It is false if the question is only about the student's topic and does not use anything the teacher said (the teacher says "Dogs need water." and asks "What is your dog's name?"), or if there is no question.

"content" is true if, before the question, the teacher says something new and real about the topic: a fact, a detail, an example or a short story. It is false if the teacher only greets, praises, repeats or rephrases the student's words, or says nothing before the question.
